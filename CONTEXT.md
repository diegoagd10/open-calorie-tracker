# Calorie Tracker Context

## Ubiquitous language

- **User** — the individual who records and reviews their own food intake.
- **Daily log** — the chronological record of a user's food entries for one calendar date.
- **Timezone** — the User's configured local timezone used to convert stored timestamps into Daily log dates and times.
- **Food entry** — one recorded consumption event in a daily log. It has a date, an optional meal context, a quantity, a nutritional snapshot, and may reference the reusable Food item used to create it.
- **Food item** — a reusable nutritional definition for a packaged product, ingredient, or saved dish.
- **Nutritional profile** — the calories and nutrients associated with one serving or declared quantity of a food item; calories are required, other nutrient values may be unknown, and the quantity basis is declared.
- **Nutritional snapshot** — the immutable nutritional values and declared quantity basis captured on a Food entry when it is logged; later changes to a reusable Food item or Meal do not rewrite it.
- **Ingredient** — a food item used as a component of a saved dish or identified by an image.
- **Food candidate** — a proposed food item or nutritional match produced by a lookup or AI-assisted flow and awaiting user confirmation.
- **Food image** — a retained image associated with the Food item or Meal it helps identify, create, or correct.
- **Meal** — a named, reusable combination of ingredients that produces a nutritional profile for one assembled Meal unit.
- **Favorite** — a user-owned Food item or Meal marked for convenient reuse in My Favorites.
- **Quantity** — the amount of a Food item or Meal expressed against its unit; it scales the nutritional profile and may be entered as a decimal or fraction.
- **Serving** — a quantity unit used to scale a nutritional profile when it is added to a daily log or Meal.
- **Meal tag** — an optional label on a food entry such as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack; it categorizes an entry without changing the chronological log.
- **Daily summary** — the calculated total of the nutritional snapshots in one daily log. It shows, in order, Calories, Protein, Total carbohydrates, Fat, Fiber, Added sugar, Total sugar, Saturated fat, and Sodium. Total carbohydrates, Added sugar, and Total sugar are independent measures, with Added sugar shown before Total sugar. Protein has no default reference in v1; reference indicators appear only when the User configures them.
- **Nutrition reference profile** — a versioned set of general targets, minimums, ranges, or upper limits for the v1 adult 19+ population; it is informational and is not medical advice.
- **Nutrition estimate** — an optional, user-visible estimate of maintenance calories and macro references for an adult 19+ User, derived from configured profile inputs; it is a general starting point, not a medical prescription.
- **Nutrition plan** — the User's selected intention to lose weight, maintain weight, or gain weight. Lose and Gain plans include a target weight and target date used to form a proposed target.
- **Nutrition target** — a daily calorie or nutrient target or range that the User has reviewed and confirmed; a proposed target is not active until confirmation.

## Model boundaries

A meal is composed of confirmed food items; a food candidate must be confirmed before it can become an ingredient. A Meal unit's nutrition is the sum of its ingredient profiles scaled by their quantities. Calculations retain full precision through aggregation and use the same final-display policy on Food detail/review, Meal editing, individual food entries, and the Daily summary: calories display as whole kcal, gram-based nutrients display to one decimal place, and milligram- or microgram-based nutrients display as whole units. Quantities use normalized fractions where applicable. A Nutrition estimate is available only for an adult 19+ User when age, the equation's sex category, height, weight, and an explicitly selected activity level (`Inactive`, `Low active`, `Active`, or `Very active`) are present; otherwise the User may enter a manual target and the app does not guess. A Nutrition plan may produce a proposed Nutrition target using a dynamic energy-balance estimate for its current weight, target weight, target date, and activity; that target becomes active only after the User reviews and confirms it. Editing a Meal affects future uses only; an existing food entry keeps the nutritional snapshot recorded for that event, while an edit made on the food entry affects only that entry. A retained timestamp is stored as UTC and converted through the User's Timezone for Daily log dates and times. Missing optional nutrient values remain unknown in Food items and Food entries, contribute zero only to Daily summary arithmetic, and are shown with a missing-data warning. Saving a Food entry as a Favorite creates an independent Food item from that entry's snapshot and quantity. Favoriting a database result likewise creates an independent Food item from its confirmed values; later source corrections do not mutate it without an explicit refresh. Favoriting an existing Meal marks that Meal, and unfavoriting any item removes only its Favorite marker without deleting or changing the item. A retained Food image belongs to the Food item or Meal it helps identify, create, or correct, rather than to every historical food entry, and remains until explicitly deleted. The first release serves one User per self-hosted deployment; that User owns the instance and its data, while authentication, synchronization, and multiple users are deferred for a future extension. A meal is reusable, while a food entry is historical.
