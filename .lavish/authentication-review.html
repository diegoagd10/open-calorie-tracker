Production blocker

Delivery and deployment

  • Which email provider meets idempotency and failure-reporting needs?
  • Where will the same-origin PWA and Express API be hosted?
  • Which Node.js LTS release is the production baseline?
  • What are the production rate limits and revoked-session retention period?
Resolved · Delivery

Safari-installed PWA

Version one installs from Safari on iPhone. The production build now emits a standalone manifest, Apple touch icon, and automatically updated service worker. Capacitor and App Store packaging are out of scope.

Resolved · Product boundary

Recovery explicitly deferred

Version one has no self-service email-address change or lost-email recovery API or UI. The PRD and TDD now state the exclusion directly.

Resolved · Quality target

WCAG 2.2 Level AA

  • Version one must conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
  • Visible focus, announced validation, text-supported states, contrast, reduced motion, and 44pt targets are implemented in the mock.
  • Automated accessibility enforcement remains a production quality task.
